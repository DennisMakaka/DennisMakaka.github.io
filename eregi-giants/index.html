Sport with
purpose.

Eregi Giants Basketball Academy is a grassroots youth empowerment initiative located in the Eregi and Ikolomani regions of Kenya.

We launched “Education Through Hoops” to address youth indiscipline, school dropouts and gang involvement—bridging social gaps, identifying talent and supporting education through sport.
